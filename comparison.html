Strim or a full playlist editor?

Strim keeps the groups you watch, gives your players one link and shows what the list holds. It does not edit single channels; if you want that, another tool fits better. Here is where the line is.

What Strim does and what it leaves out

Strim does

  • Load M3U and M3U8 links, Xtream Codes get.php links included
  • List every group with its size and type: live, movies or series
  • Split groups that mix live channels and series
  • Switch whole groups on and off, one by one or all visible at once
  • Find any film, series or channel and the group it is in
  • Report duplicates and quality variants
  • Download the cleaned .m3u, no account needed
  • Keep a player link that follows the provider’s list and answers with the last list that worked
  • Show the subscription’s expiry, status and connections for get.php links
  • Open a read-only Library with marks and a watchlist, shared with up to 10 people
  • Put the watchlist in the player as the first group

Strim does not

  • Rename, reorder or delete single channels
  • Change logos, EPG IDs or other channel attributes
  • Edit or merge EPG guides
  • Combine several provider lists into one
  • Remove duplicates: it only reports them
  • Host, relay, play or sell any streams

Which one you need

Strim is enough when

Your provider’s list is mostly fine, just far too long. You want to drop whole countries, the movie and series catalogue or adult groups, and have your player pick up new channels in the groups you kept. You also want to see what the list holds and remember what you watched.

A full editor fits better when

You want to build your own channel order, rename channels, fix logos or EPG mappings, or merge lists from several providers. Those editors work per channel; Strim only works per group.
