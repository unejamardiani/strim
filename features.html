Features

Everything Strim does, nothing it doesn’t.

Strim takes the playlist your IPTV provider sends, lets you switch off the groups you never watch and gives your player a clean list. The Library lets you find films and series in that list, and sharing lets the people you watch with browse it too. Here is all of it.

Loading a playlist

From your provider’s link

Paste the M3U or M3U8 URL (Xtream Codes get.php links work too). Strim downloads it on the server, so your phone never has to parse a 50 MB file.

Large lists welcome

Lists are streamed to disk and read line by line. Lists with tens of thousands of channels, films and episodes work; up to 10,000 groups are supported. A very large list can take up to a minute.

Try it with a sample

No link at hand? Load the built-in sample playlist and see how the editor works before you paste your own.

Picking groups

Every group in the playlist appears once, with its number of entries and a switch.

Live, movies and series

Strim recognises the content type of every entry and marks each group:

Live Movies Series

Mixed groups split

Providers often reuse one group name for live channels and series. Strim splits such a group into “Germany” and “Germany · Series”, so you can keep one and drop the other.

Search, filter, sort

  • Search group names as you type
  • Show only live, movies or series
  • Show only groups that are on or off
  • Original order or largest first

All on, all off

Switch every visible group at once; the button shows how many groups it changes. Filter to “Movies”, press “All off” and every film group is gone. Undo puts them back.

Find any title

  • Search every channel, film and episode with Everything
  • Jump from a match to its group
  • Language labels from tags in the titles
  • Keep only the languages you choose

Hide adult groups

When the list has adult groups, one switch turns them all off. Each one stays a normal group you can switch back on.

Duplicates & variants

A report of the titles that appear more than once and the variants of the same title. It only reports; it removes nothing.

More room on a wide screen

On a laptop the groups and the entries of the selected group sit side by side, so you see what a group holds before you switch it.

Player link

Download .m3u

Get the cleaned file straight away, no account needed. The header line with the programme-guide address, every attribute and the order stay as the provider wrote them; only the movies and series of a split group get their new group name.

Player link

Sign in to save a playlist and get a permanent link for your IPTV app. Each time the player refreshes, Strim reads the provider’s current list and applies your choices.

When the provider is down

If the provider or Strim has a problem, the player link answers with the last list that worked instead of an error.

Link controls

  • Turn the link off and on
  • Replace it with a new link if it leaks

Refresh every 6 hours

Switch it on and Strim re-checks the provider every six hours, keeping group counts current and the next player refresh fast.

Watchlist group

Your watchlist, and that of the people you share with, as the first groups in your player: “Watchlist” and “Watchlist · Series”. One switch per playlist, off by default.

Facts about your subscription

What the television apps do not tell you, on each saved playlist.

Expiry date

When the provider link carries an expiry date, Strim shows it, warns you in the last two weeks and says when the subscription has ended.

Account status and connections

For Xtream Codes get.php links, Strim shows when the account is not active and how many of its connections are in use.

Last fetched

See when a player last fetched the link, so you know your television still reads it.

The Library

Every saved playlist has a Library to find films and series in it. It is read-only: nothing you do there changes the playlist. It shows the groups that are on; “Include switched-off groups” shows the full list. With TMDB on, Strim sends TMDB only a cleaned title and its year; the Library works without it.

Search

Find films and series as you type. A series appears as one row, not one row per episode.

Browse groups

Filter and sort the groups. A group that grew since your last visit on this device shows “+N new”.

Marks and watchlist

Mark a title as seen, give it a thumbs up or down, or put it on your watchlist. Marks belong to each person.

Home

Your watchlist, the series you follow and what is new in your groups, on one page.

Film and series cards

Open a title to see what it is and walk to similar ones, where the server has a TMDB key.

Posters and suggestions

Posters and the rails “Trending this week” and “Because you liked …”, built from titles in your list, where the server has a TMDB key.

Sharing

Invite up to 10 people

Share a saved playlist with the people you watch with.

The invite link

You send the link yourself. It works once and runs out after 7 days.

What they get

The Library of your list: browse, search, open a title, and their own marks and watchlist.

What stays with you

The provider link, the player link and every change to the list. Only you edit it.

Accounts and privacy

Optional accounts

A username and password is all it takes. Sign-in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub is available where the server has it configured.

Your name and look

Choose a display name and an avatar, and pick the appearance: light, dark or the system’s.

Easy to read and to reach

  • Light and dark themes
  • The system’s “more contrast” and forced colours
  • Large touch targets
  • A visible keyboard focus

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no third-party scripts or fonts. Fonts are served by Strim itself. With TMDB on, posters load from TMDB’s image host.

Your provider login stays private

A player link contains a random code, never your provider URL or login. Temporary files are deleted automatically.

Open source

The code is on GitHub. Run your own copy with Docker and SQLite or PostgreSQL.
