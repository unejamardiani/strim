Features

Everything Strim does, nothing it doesn’t.

Strim takes the playlist your IPTV provider sends, lets you switch off the groups you never watch and gives your player a clean list. Here is all of it.

Loading a playlist

From your provider’s link

Paste the M3U or M3U8 URL (Xtream Codes get.php links work too). Strim downloads it on the server, so your phone never has to parse a 50 MB file.

Large lists welcome

Lists are streamed to disk and read line by line. Tens of thousands of channels, films and episodes load in seconds; up to 10,000 groups are supported.

Try it with a sample

No link at hand? Load the built-in sample playlist and see how the editor works before you paste your own.

Subscription date

When the provider link carries an expiry date, Strim shows it and warns you once the subscription has ended.

Picking groups

Every group in the playlist appears once, with its number of entries and a switch.

Live, movies and series

Strim recognises the content type of every entry and marks each group:

Live Movies Series

Mixed groups split

Providers often reuse one group name for live channels and series. Strim splits such a group into “Germany” and “Germany · Series”, so you can keep one and drop the other.

Search, filter, sort

  • Search group names as you type
  • Show only live, movies or series
  • Show only groups that are on or off
  • Original order or largest first

All on, all off

Switch every visible group at once. Filter to “Movies”, press “All off” and every film group is gone.

Using the result

Download .m3u

Get the cleaned file straight away, no account needed. Channel names, logos, EPG IDs and other attributes stay as the provider wrote them; only the movies and series of a split group get their new group name.

Player link

Sign in to save a playlist and get a permanent link for your IPTV app. Each time the player refreshes, Strim reads the provider’s current list and applies your choices.

Link controls

  • Turn the link off and on
  • Replace it with a new link if it leaks
  • See when a player last fetched it

Auto-refresh

Switch on auto-refresh and Strim re-checks the provider every six hours, keeping channel counts current and the next player refresh fast.

Accounts and privacy

Optional accounts

A username and password is all it takes. Sign-in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub is available where the server has it configured.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no third-party scripts or fonts. Fonts are served by Strim itself.

Your provider login stays private

A player link contains a random code, never your provider URL or login. Temporary files are deleted automatically.

Open source

The code is on GitHub. Run your own copy with Docker and SQLite or PostgreSQL.
