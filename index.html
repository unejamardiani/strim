Playlist editor and library

Pick it on the phone. Find it on the TV.

Strim trims the playlist your IPTV provider sends, gives your players one link that stays current, and turns what is left into a library you can search, mark and share.

  1. Clean

    Switch off what you never watch.

    Paste the provider’s link. Strim reads it on the server and lists every group with its size and type, so a list of tens of thousands of entries stays quick on a phone.

    • Live, movies and series apart. A mixed group is split, so you keep “Germany” and drop “Germany · Series”.
    • Search everything. Find a film, series or channel and jump to its group.
    • Safe bulk changes. All on and All off say how many groups they touch and can be undone.
  2. Link

    One link for every player.

    Save the playlist and give the player link to TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, VLC, Kodi or any app that takes an M3U address. Each refresh starts from the provider’s current list and applies your choices.

    • Facts your TV app hides. Subscription end, connections, and when a player last fetched the link.
    • Your watchlist as the first group. One switch per playlist.
    • Yours to switch off. Turn the link off, or replace it if it leaks.
  3. Library

    Know what the list holds.

    Every saved playlist has a read-only Library. Search films, series and channels, browse by group, and open a card with poster, rating, seasons and the similar titles that are in your list.

    • Marks. Seen, thumbs up, thumbs down and a watchlist, kept per person.
    • Suggestions from your own list. Trending this week and “Because you liked”.
    • One row per series. With the seasons and episodes your list has.

    Posters, cards and suggestions use TMDB and appear where the server has a TMDB key.

  4. Together

    Share the library, keep the keys.

    Invite up to ten people to a playlist’s Library. They browse with their own account and keep their own marks. The provider link and the player link stay with you.

    • An invite link you send yourself. It works once and for seven days.
    • Read-only. Nothing an invited person does changes the list.
    • Shared watchlist on the TV. Their bookmarks join the Watchlist group, without names.

Questions

Do I need an account?

Not to clean a list: load a playlist, pick groups and download the file as a guest. The player link, the Library, marks and sharing need an account.

Does Strim host or play any streams?

No. Strim edits the playlist you bring and shows what is in it. The streams stay with your provider, and your player plays them from there.

What if my provider is down?

The player link answers with the last list that worked, so your player keeps its channels until the provider is back.

Where do posters and film details come from?

From TMDB, on servers that have a TMDB key set up. Strim sends only a cleaned title and year, never your provider link. Without a key the Library works without posters and cards.

What can an invited person see?

The Library of that one playlist, read-only, with their own marks and watchlist. Never the provider link, the player link or your marks.
