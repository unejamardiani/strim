Your playlist, minus the noise.

IPTV providers send thousands of channels, movies and series you will never watch. Paste your provider’s link, switch off the groups you don’t want and give your player one clean link that stays up to date.

Three steps, about a minute

  1. Paste your link

    The M3U or M3U8 link from your provider. Strim reads it on the server, so even lists with tens of thousands of entries load without freezing your phone.

  2. Switch off what you skip

    Every group is listed with its size. Search, filter by live, movies or series, and switch off whole groups at once.

  3. Play it anywhere

    Download the cleaned .m3u, or sign in and copy the player link into TiviMate, IPTV Smarters, VLC, Kodi or any app that takes an M3U URL.

Built for the lists providers actually send

Strim is a small tool with one job: keep the groups you watch and drop the rest, reliably.

Live, movies and series apart

Many providers put live channels and series episodes under the same group name. Strim splits them, so you can keep “Germany” live and drop “Germany · Series”.

A link that stays current

Your player link reads the provider’s list each time it is opened and applies your choices. New channels in the groups you kept simply appear.

You stay in control

Turn a link off, or replace it with a new one if it ever leaks. Strim shows when a player last fetched it.

Fast on huge lists

Playlists are streamed to disk and filtered on the server. A list fetched moments ago is reused, and providers that support it only resend a list when it changed.

Made for the sofa

Big switches, a light and a dark theme, and a layout that works one-handed on a phone.

Free and open source

No ads, no tracking. The code is on GitHub and runs anywhere Docker runs, with SQLite or PostgreSQL.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can load a playlist, pick groups and download the cleaned file as a guest. An account is only needed for a saved playlist and its player link.

Does Strim host or sell any streams?

No. Strim only edits the playlist you bring. The streams stay with your provider, and the player plays them from there.

What happens when my provider adds channels?

The player link always starts from the provider’s current list. Channels added to groups you kept appear on the player’s next refresh; groups you switched off stay off.

Is my provider link safe?

The link usually contains your provider login, so treat it like a password. Strim stores it only when you save a playlist, never shows it on the player link, and lets you replace a player link at any time.
