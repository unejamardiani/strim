How to Use Strim

Filter, organize, and clean your IPTV playlist in a few simple steps. Works with any M3U source.

01

Load Your Playlist

Open Strim in your browser. No installation or sign-up required. You can either paste your M3U URL into the input box and click Fetch, or paste the raw M3U content directly into the text area.

02

Review Groups

After loading, your playlist is automatically parsed and displayed in the sidebar grouped by category. Each group shows a count of channels it contains. The input tab displays the total channel count and group list.

03

Select Channels to Keep

Check the groups you want to keep. Use the select/deselect all toggle to quickly manage your selection. The filter tab updates in real-time as you make changes. You can also use the search bar to find specific groups.

04

Generate Cleaned Playlist

Click Generate in the output tab to create your new filtered playlist. Strim processes everything client-side and generates a clean M3U file with only your selected groups.

05

Save or Share

Download the cleaned playlist as an .m3u file, copy it to your clipboard, or generate a shareable URL for your IPTV app. The share URL can be refreshed automatically to pick up changes.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use URL playlists if possible — they allow automatic refresh support via shareable links.
  • Start with select all, then deselect the groups you do not want.
  • Check the channel count per group to focus on the largest groups first.
  • CORS issues? Start the included CORS proxy or use the raw paste option.
  • Bookmark the share URL for one-click access to your filtered playlist.
