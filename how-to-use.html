How to use

From provider link to clean player list.

You need the M3U link from your IPTV provider and about a minute. Here is the whole flow, step by step.

  1. Open Strim and paste your link

    Open the app and paste the M3U or M3U8 URL your provider gave you, then press Load groups. Xtream Codes links that look like …/get.php?username=…&password=…&type=m3u_plus work as they are.

    Large playlists can take up to a minute. No link yet? Choose Try a sample to explore with a made-up playlist.

  2. Switch off the groups you never watch

    Every group is listed with its number of entries and a switch. Its marker shows what it contains: a circle for live TV, a diamond for movies, a square for series.

    • Type in the search box to find a group by name.
    • Use the Live, Movies and Series pills to show one type, then All off to drop all of it.
    • Sort by Largest to find the groups that make your list slow.

    The bar at the bottom shows how many entries are still on.

  3. Download, or save for a player link

    Download gives you the cleaned .m3u file right away, no account needed. It is a snapshot: it will not pick up changes from your provider.

    To get a link that stays current, sign in and press Save. Strim gives the playlist a player link; each time your player refreshes, Strim reads the provider’s current list and applies your choices.

  4. Add the player link to your IPTV app

    Press Copy on the player link and add it as an M3U playlist URL:

    • TiviMate: Settings → Playlists → Add playlist → Enter URL.
    • IPTV Smarters: Add user → Load your playlist or file/URL.
    • VLC: Media → Open Network Stream.
    • Kodi: PVR IPTV Simple Client → M3U play list URL.

  5. Change it whenever you like

    Open the playlist in Strim, switch groups on or off and press Save. Players get the change on their next refresh; the link itself stays the same.

    From the playlist’s menu you can turn the link off, create a new link (the old one stops working), switch on auto-refresh or delete the playlist.

If something does not work

“The source returned HTTP 403”

Some providers block requests from servers. Check the link works in a browser on your own network, or ask your provider whether it allows server access.

The player shows no channels

Check that the link is turned on in Strim and that your subscription has not expired. Strim shows the expiry date when the provider link contains one.

A group is missing

Use the Off filter to list every group you switched off. Groups your provider removes disappear from Strim as well.
